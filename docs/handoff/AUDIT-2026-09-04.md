### C.4 Proposta di rampa tipografica (lane R1-type-static, riportata per intero dal suo summary)

> PROPOSED TYPE RAMP (px today -> rem-based; 1rem = 16 at default, scales with OS setting; leading/colour per breakpoint; phone <720, tablet 720-1179, desktop 1180+):
> - Rules/feature/card text (today .t-dense 11.5 and .t-read 13, both READ): one role `.t-read` = 1rem/1.5 on phone (16px), 0.9375rem/1.5 tablet+desktop (15px), ink --text-2. Reason: this is the text a player reads with the phone at 35-45cm under a table lamp; at 11.5px Archivo x-height is ~6px, below what an uncorrected presbyope resolves at that distance. Retire .t-dense for prose; keep it (renamed .t-hint, 0.8125rem = 13px, --text-3) only for hint paragraphs under a control.
> - Long prose (.t-body): 1.0625rem/1.55 on phone (17px), 1rem/1.5 tablet+desktop, --text-2 on dark (9.67) and --text-3 on light (8.19) — dark rooms want the brighter ink; cap 62ch everywhere including StatBlock in Bestiary.
> - Secondary/meta (.t-meta): 0.75rem (12px) on phone, 0.6875rem (11px) desktop, keep Plex 500, drop tracking to 0.04em; never below 12px where it carries a sentence (role=status, "NO MOVES CHOSEN…").
> - Headings/labels (.t-label): 0.6875rem (11px) 600 caps 0.12em max; any label longer than ~3 words goes to sentence case in .t-hint. Tab labels (TabBar:295) 0.75rem 700.
> - Chips (.chip): 0.6875rem (11px) on any-pointer:coarse, 0.625rem (10px) fine pointer; chips carrying names (experiences, tags) use 0.75rem. Chip height comes from padding, not a fixed height, so the touch floor is unchanged.
> - Numbers glanced (.t-vital/.t-roll/--counter-num): unchanged in px — they are glanced from arm's length, already 21-56px, and their cells are measured in px; converting them would break the counter-cell arithmetic for no legibility gain.
> - Controls (.btn) 0.875rem (14px) 600; inputs already 16px on coarse pointers — extend that rule to any-pointer: coarse (see R1-9).
> - Print: body 9pt/1.35, .dhc-meta 7.5pt, labels 7pt, footer 7pt; nothing under 7pt on paper.
>
> Ergonomics: text that is READ sits in the middle of the screen (card reader, statblock, Play loadout) and is read with the phone held, not thumbed — size and leading pay off there and cost only scroll, which Play now allows by decision. Text that is TOUCHED (chips, tabs, steppers, keypad) sits in the thumb arc at the bottom 40% of the phone; there the 44px target is the ergonomic term and the label is glanced, so 11-12px with 600 weight is enough and larger type would wrap and reflow the strip (see the FavorRow/Counter docblocks). Hence two different floors: 16px for read, 11px for glance-in-the-arc.
